Rim rate is the target's spin about the cone axis. In blocks C to H2 the target arm drives the rim at the set rate. In block P it obeys I dω/dt = τ, with τ measured at the chaser wrist (free-spin emulation). Fig. 4 and Table 2 use the 160 trials of blocks C, S and O. Fig. 5 uses the 54 holds of G and G2 at closing speed 255. The law is fitted on the 48 sweeps of H and H2; Fig. 6 shows the 30 at closing speed 255. Fig. 7 tests the law on the 220 holds of C, S, G and G2. All trials made contact. Both arms were recorded at 500 Hz.
ᵃ Rigid hand: release after 2.0 s, delayed while the rim still turns in the wedging direction (measured 2.0 to 2.8 s). Damping hand: release once the torque is below 0.15 N m and the spin below 1 deg/s, after at least 1.0 s (measured 1.0 to 2.4 s). Both: release at 3.5 s at the latest.
