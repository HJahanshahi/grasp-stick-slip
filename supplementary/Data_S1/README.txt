Data S1. Data underlying the figures.
Jahanshahi and Zhu, "A stiff robotic grasp stores and returns the spin of a captured body through asymmetric stick-slip".

  DataS1_Fig3.csv      Fig. 3, panels A and B

Layout of the figure files
  Each row is one plotted point: panel, series_no, x, y.
  The pair (panel, series_no) identifies a series in DataS1_index.csv.
  Points of a line are listed in the order in which they are drawn.

Notes
  Fig. 2 is a set of photographs. Panels A and B of Fig. 1 and panel A of fig. S5 are drawings. They have no data.
  Axis lines, reference lines and shaded bands are left out.
  "hardware" is a measurement and "law" is the fitted law driven as described in the paper.
  In the panels that show single trials, "positive" and "negative" give the sign of the starting spin.
  A mean is stored as the two end points of the short bar that marks it in the figure.
  In Fig. 3A the rim rate of each hold is given in the series name.
  Units are N·m, deg, deg/s, s, N and kg·m². Finger speed and travel are in the position units of the hand.
  Files are UTF-8 text with comma separators.
